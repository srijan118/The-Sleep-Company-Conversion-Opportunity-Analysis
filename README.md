# The Sleep Company – Conversion Opportunity Analysis

**Improving Mattress Discovery Through Guided Product Recommendations**  
*Product Management Case Study | Mobile Website*

## Overview

An independent conversion opportunity analysis of **The Sleep Company**, focused on its mobile website homepage and the existing Mattress Finder experience.

While exploring the website, I identified an opportunity to improve how first-time visitors discover and use the Mattress Finder feature.

The objective was to reduce friction in the mattress recommendation journey, improve product discovery, and potentially increase purchase conversion.

## Opportunity Identified

The Sleep Company already offers a Mattress Finder feature that helps users receive personalized mattress recommendations through WhatsApp.

However, the existing experience introduces two friction points:

- The value proposition of Mattress Finder is not clearly communicated to first-time visitors.
- Users are redirected from the website to WhatsApp to complete the recommendation journey.

This creates an opportunity to improve feature discoverability and keep users within the website experience.

## Current vs Proposed User Journey

**Current Flow**

Homepage → Discover Mattress Finder → Redirect to WhatsApp → Answer Questions → Receive Recommendation

**Proposed Flow**

Homepage → "Find Your Perfect Mattress in 30 Seconds" → In-App Quiz → Personalized Recommendation → Product Page

## Proposed Product Improvement

I recommended repositioning Mattress Finder as a prominent homepage CTA and integrating the recommendation journey directly into the website.

The proposed experience aims to:

- Make Mattress Finder more discoverable above the fold.
- Communicate its value proposition clearly.
- Eliminate unnecessary external redirection.
- Guide users through a personalized mattress selection journey.
- Improve confidence during a high-consideration purchase decision.

## Expected Impact

**User Impact**
- Reduced cognitive load during product selection.
- Increased confidence in purchase decisions.
- Faster identification of suitable products.

**Business Impact**
- Higher engagement with Mattress Finder.
- Improved click-through rates to product pages.
- Increased add-to-cart rates.
- Higher conversion among first-time visitors.

*These are expected outcomes, not measured results.*

## Validation Approach

I proposed an A/B test comparing:

- **Control:** Existing mobile homepage and WhatsApp-based Mattress Finder journey.
- **Variant:** Homepage with a prominent CTA and an integrated in-app recommendation experience.

**Key Metrics**

- Mattress Finder initiation rate.
- Quiz completion rate.
- Product page click-through rate.
- Add-to-cart rate.
- Purchase conversion rate.

## Key Takeaway

The opportunity is not necessarily to build an entirely new feature, but to make an existing solution easier to discover and use.

For high-consideration purchases like mattresses, guided product discovery can reduce decision fatigue, improve user confidence, and potentially shorten the path to purchase.
